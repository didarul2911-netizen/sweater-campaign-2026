## 📋 Campaign Overview

Under the **Exium MUPS 4Q'26 Doctor Sweater Campaign**, premium sweaters (manufactured by Richman / Lubnan Trade Consortium Ltd.) are allocated across **1,856 Territories** in **252 Regions** and **35 Zones**.

### Total Volume:
* **8 Sweaters per Territory** &times; **1,856 Territories** = **14,848 Sweaters**.

## 🔐 How to Log In & Submit Data

1. Open the **[Live Portal](https://didarul2911-netizen.github.io/sweater-campaign-2026/)**.
2. **Step 1:** Select your **Zone** (from 35 Zones).
3. **Step 2:** Select your **Region** (from 252 Regions). Your name as Regional Head will appear automatically.
4. **Step 3:** Enter your **5-digit SAP Region Code** as your password.
5. Fill up the doctor names and sweater selections for all territories under your region.
6. Click **Save Territory** after filling each territory.
7. Click **Next** to move to the next territory.
